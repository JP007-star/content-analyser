# JP News 360 — Voice Profile

## Channel Identity
- **Name**: JP News 360
- **Handle**: @jp-news-360
- **Niche**: TTD Tirupati Darshan updates, IPOs & Stock Market, Cinema, Cricket & Sports, Trending News
- **Languages**: Tamil (primary) + English (financial/technical terms)
- **Posting**: Daily Shorts (multiple topics) + Event-driven long-form

## Core Concept
**"Your World, One Update at a Time!"** — Multi-niche news hub covering what matters to Tamil audiences: spiritual (TTD), financial (IPOs/stocks), entertainment (cinema), sports (cricket), and viral trends. Fast, factual, formatted for the Shorts feed.

## Tone & Style
| Dimension | Approach |
|-----------|----------|
| **Energy** | Urgent but trustworthy, "breaking news" cadence |
| **Pacing** | Rapid-fire for Shorts, structured for long-form |
| **Voice** | Tamil narration + English keywords (IPO, NSE, TTD, Box Office) |
| **Perspective** | "Insider access" — temple queues, listing gains, opening day collections |

## Hook Formulas (Priority Order)
1. **TTD Live Alert** — "TTD Slot 1: 74 pilgrims waiting RIGHT NOW. Here's how to book."
2. **IPO Listing Day** — "This IPO listed at 40% premium. ₹10k became ₹14k in 1 hour."

5. **Trending Viral Decode** — "Everyone's sharing this video. The truth behind the trend."

## Format DNA

### Shorts (30-60s) — Multi-topic Daily
```
[0-1s]  CATEGORY ICON + HEADLINE — 🔱 TTD / 📈 IPO / 🎬 Cinema / 🏏 Cricket / 🔥 Trending
[1-3s]  THE NUMBER — "74 pilgrims" / "40% premium" / "₹50Cr" / "6 wickets"
[3-10s] CONTEXT — One sentence in Tamil + English terms
[10-25s] VISUAL PROOF — Screenshot / queue cam / chart / scoreboard / tweet
[25-40s] ACTIONABLE — How to book / apply / watch / verify
[40-50s] CTA — "Full details in description" / "Part 2 at 6 PM"
```

### Long-form (3-5 min) — Event Deep Dives
- **TTD Special**: Complete darshan guide, SSD token calendar, accommodation hacks
- **IPO Analysis**: DRHP breakdown, GMP tracking, listing strategy, allocation math
- **Cinema Review**: Box office trajectory, audience verdict, trade analysis
- **Cricket Series**: Match highlights + tactical breakdown + fantasy picks
- **Trend Investigation**: Viral claim → fact-check → source trace

## Audience Profile
- **Primary**: Tamil-speaking adults (25-55) — employed, investing, temple-going, cinema-loving
- **Secondary**: NRIs tracking India news, students following cricket/cinema
- **Pain points**: TTD booking confusion, IPO scams, fake box office numbers, cricket misinformation
- **Aspirations**: Stress-free darshan, smart investing, informed fan, trend-literate

## Content Guardrails
- ❌ No financial advice — "GMP indicates" not "will list at"
- ❌ No unverified TTD rumors — only official sources
- ❌ No box office estimates without trade source citation
- ❌ No cricket betting references — fantasy only
- ❌ No clickbait thumbnails mismatching content
- ✅ Every number sourced (official website, exchange filing, trade tracker)
- ✅ Tamil + English in every video
- ✅ Actionable takeaway: link, date, process, deadline
- ✅ Correction policy: pin comment + next video acknowledgment

3. **Cinema video → next-day view retention** — sustained interest
4. **Cricket video → fantasy team shares** — engagement
5. **Trend video → share rate** — viral amplification
6. **Cross-category watch** — viewer watching 3+ niches = loyal subscriber
